# Links catalogue

## Hiking and extreme sports

[http://www.mountain.ru/](http://www.mountain.ru/) A popular mountaineering portal. European section: [http://www.mountain.ru/world\_mounts/western\_europe/](http://www.mountain.ru/world_mounts/western_europe/).

[https://www.risk.ru/](https://www.risk.ru/) A portal about mountain tourism and extreme sports. Its description: "Risk.ru is about people whose lives are connected with mountains; about mountain sports and leisure; and, of course, about mountains!"

[http://www.trekkingclub.ru](http://www.trekkingclub.ru) Nizhny Novgorod hiking club website. Its members describe it as having become the country's largest and most successful mountain club in nine years. It offers worldwide trip reports and reference materials. Related websites: [http://www.turpohod.org/](http://www.turpohod.org/) [http://www.fotogor.ru/](http://www.fotogor.ru/).

[http://skitalets.ru](http://skitalets.ru) A website about travel and travelers, with a large library of materials, older literature and hiking stories. After a long period of neglect, it was revived in the year described by this overview.

[http://eurotourist.club/](http://eurotourist.club/) A Ukrainian hiking forum with a long and complicated history, whose popularity peaked in the preceding decade. Its former address was [https://travel.org.ua](https://travel.org.ua). It contains many archived discussions. Its history, which unfolded before our eyes, illustrates two distinct attractions of hiking: for some it is a way to recharge before returning to active social life; for others it is an escape from life, a chance to "bury their heads in the sand".

[http://www.kombrig.net](http://www.kombrig.net/vstuplenie_v_alpklub.htm) A personal website about hiking in the Alps, among the most comprehensive and authoritative on the subject. [http://ru-alps.livejournal.com/](http://ru-alps.livejournal.com/) A LiveJournal community about Alpine trekking, founded and maintained by the same author, with interesting archived materials and discussions.

[![](../images/pixabay/eagle-1009771_640.jpg)](../images/pixabay/eagle-1009771_1280.jpg) [Image by chezbeate from Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/photos/eagle-majestic-animal-bird-freedom-1009771/)

[http://www.kovinov.com](http://www.kovinov.com) Dmitry Kovinov's personal travel website, covering the world with particular attention to Sochi. European travel section: [http://www.kovinov.com/europe/](http://www.kovinov.com/europe/). His articles also draw on professional experience in outdoor retail. Equipment section: [http://www.kovinov.com/snarjazhenie/](http://www.kovinov.com/snarjazhenie/).

[https://trekkingmania.ru](https://trekkingmania.ru) Original articles about travel and equipment, describing the project as "a nationwide game for outdoor enthusiasts", with many lively first-hand accounts.

[http://legkohod.com](http://legkohod.com) Legkohod.com shares experience and information about lightweight hiking in Russian. It challenges the post-Soviet stereotype that hiking requires carrying an 80-litre backpack weighing at least 20 kg. The site explains how new materials, methods, knowledge and skills make lighter hiking possible, introduces ultralight principles and specific equipment, and shares practical trip experience.

[http://poxod.ru/](http://poxod.ru/) Independent tourism portal: technical trip reports, equipment, learning materials and route selection.

[http://www.vvv.ru/](http://www.vvv.ru/) Extreme sports directory covering diving, downhill biking, hang gliding, kiteboarding, kitesurfing, kayaking, ice climbing and more.

[https://www.ski.ru/](https://www.ski.ru/) A skiing portal, particularly about Alpine resorts, with some mountain hiking material.

[http://alps-trip.livejournal.com/](http://alps-trip.livejournal.com/) LiveJournal community about trekking in the Alps.

[http://kovrik-super.ru/index.php/otchety](http://kovrik-super.ru/index.php/otchety) Personal website with hiking reports from Europe and Russia.

[https://www.summitpost.org](https://www.summitpost.org) SummitPost is a community focused on climbing, mountaineering, hiking and other outdoor activities. Members contribute photos, trip reports, events, logs and albums, share expertise through articles and information pages, and help select useful content by voting on submissions.

## Tour operators and commercial hiking trips

Adventure Club (Russia, Moscow and St Petersburg): [https://www.vpoxod.ru/allroutes](https://www.vpoxod.ru/allroutes). Commercial trips worldwide. Its description: "Travel is Adventure Club! People planning an active holiday know where to turn. Their trust motivates us to keep developing and ensure reliable, high-quality service."

PIK hiking club (Russia, St Petersburg): [https://turclub-pik.ru/](https://turclub-pik.ru/search/). Commercial hikes. Its description: "We are professional hikers who love the mountains. We lead people on hikes, show them nature's beauty and help them believe in themselves. Hiking is open to everyone: all you need is the desire."

Marshrut hiking club (Ukraine): [https://www.marshrut-club.com/calendar](https://www.marshrut-club.com/calendar). Trips to Iceland, Georgia, Turkey, Ukraine, Romania, France, Italy, Switzerland and elsewhere. The club describes a decade of expanding routes through Georgia, Turkey, Cyprus, Montenegro, Bulgaria, Romania, the Alps and Carpathians, plus trekking in Iceland, Norway and Nepal.

Alpissimo (Russia, St Petersburg): [https://alpissimo.ru/tour/trekking\_calendar.php](https://alpissimo.ru/tour/trekking_calendar.php). Commercial Alpine trekking, including independent self-guided tours. The company describes itself as a professional organizer of Alpine hikes, with routes created in 2010 and a reputation among hikers from many Russian cities.

[http://svastour.ru/](http://svastour.ru/) A Moscow tour operator offering commercial hikes in Russia. It describes its specialty as comfortable walking and mountain holidays.

[http://www.rusadventures.ru](http://www.rusadventures.ru) A Russian portal for active holidays, adventure tours and tourism, operating since 2004. It offers destination guides for Russia and other countries, plus a large directory of tours and operators.

## Travel websites

![](../images/pixabay/goat-546167_640.jpg)

[http://forum.awd.ru/](http://forum.awd.ru/) Vinsky Forum, a large Russian travel forum focused mainly on overseas trips. It has many practical and reference sections, including visa information. Most user material concerns conventional tourism, but interesting hiking reports have also appeared.

[https://forum.piligrim.ua/](https://forum.piligrim.ua/) Piligrim, a Ukrainian travel agency network with an interesting forum, including a section for finding travel companions.

[http://www.nodima.ru/](http://www.nodima.ru/) Dmitry Novitsky's family travel website, covering Europe, Russia and trips with children, plus material about emigrating to Germany.

[http://welcomeworld.ru/](http://welcomeworld.ru/) Independent Travelers Club. Yuri and Ekaterina Fedorov describe their aim as teaching people to travel independently, economically, comfortably and safely, drawing on visits to over sixty countries on five continents and an extensive training program.

[http://rukivboki.ru](http://rukivboki.ru) Ruki-v-boki, a personal website for independent travelers.

[https://turbina.ru/](https://turbina.ru/) Trip reports. Turbina describes itself as a community united by an interest in the world and travel in the broadest sense.

[https://nosviatores.com/](https://nosviatores.com/) Articles and reports about travel in Russia, Europe and the Americas. The authors describe themselves as ordinary people whose hobby and passion is travel, already planning their next trip before the current one ends.

[http://my-roads.blogspot.com/](http://my-roads.blogspot.com/) My Roads, a personal travel website with practical advice.

[https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCWelL2bSyTRzIxa7Nq13sAw](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCWelL2bSyTRzIxa7Nq13sAw) Go Alone, the personal channel of Viktoria Raevskaya, a director, camera operator, editor and traveler.
Go Alone: all about trekking. Video diary, special episode.

## International resources

[https://www.alpenverein.de/](https://www.alpenverein.de/) The German Alpine Union. Deutscher Alpenverein (DAV)

[http://www.alpenverein.at](http://www.alpenverein.at/portal/index.php) The Austrian Alpine Union. Österreichischer Alpenverein (ÖAV)

[https://www.alpenvereinaktiv.com/en/](https://www.alpenvereinaktiv.com/en/) alpenvereinaktiv.com is the joint tour portal of Deutscher Alpenverein (DAV), Austrian Alpine Club (ÖAV) and Alpenverein Südtirol (AVS). It is an information platform for (mountain) tours and open to all, regardless of whether or not an Alpine Club membership exists. The focus of the portal is on mountain businesses such as hiking, mountaineering, alpine tours, climbing, mountain biking, ski touring and snowshoeing in the Eastern Alps.

[https://www.sac-cas.ch/](https://www.sac-cas.ch/) The Swiss Alpine union

[http://www.cai.it/](http://www.cai.it/) The Italian Alpine union

[![](../images/pixabay/eagle-1009771_640.jpg)](../images/pixabay/eagle-1009771_1280.jpg) [Image by chezbeate from Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/photos/eagle-majestic-animal-bird-freedom-1009771/)

[https://www.bergfex.com/](https://www.bergfex.com/) Bergfex.at is an Austrian online platform for mountaineering and Alpine sports, the largest in Europe in this sector. The site covers more than 1,700 ski resorts in twelve European countries and 525 tourist regions in seven countries. It offers destination information, lift operating hours, accommodation, snow and weather reports, detailed maps, webcam links and other information for excursions and holidays. It also describes hiking and cycling routes, ranging from family walks to challenging tours, as well as local attractions. The portal is available in 18 languages (bergfex.at, bergfex.ch, bergfex.de, bergfex.it, bergfex.fr, and others).

[http://www.wandermap.net/](http://www.wandermap.net/) The interactive map with the user foot routes (worldwide, but the greatest quantity in Europe)


[http://www.via-alpina.org/](http://www.via-alpina.org/) The Via Alpina: a network of 5 walking trails across the eight countries of the Alpine region, more than 5000km long and with 342 day stages. Find here the official description of all the stages, a wide selection of tourist offers and places of natural and cultural interest, the real life experiences of many hikers and additional information to help prepare your Alpine trip on line.
