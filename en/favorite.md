# Ideas for hiking. Selected destinations and attractions

There are countless regions around the world suitable for hiking. For now, our site focuses on the Alps, but even there the possibilities are enormous, so we provide only brief [reference information](places/list) for each region. Where should someone new to the Alps begin? To help, we have prepared this selection of regions. It draws primarily on our own hiking experience, places we have planned or hope to visit, and our favorite trip reports found online.

## Europe

### The Alps: general information

In Europe, borders between countries are barely noticeable, making it easy to enjoy independent travel. Despite the high population density, there are good opportunities to get close to nature and spend time alone, especially high in the mountains among the Alpine meadows where edelweiss blooms in spring. Of course, the world has wilder, less populated places offering even greater freedom, but reaching them often means leaving civilization behind. In the Alps, we can learn to be free while remaining within civilization.

#### Near Vienna

[https://www.myrafaelle.at/](https://www.myrafaelle.at/) Myrafälle is a series of waterfalls in a gorge near Muggendorf in Lower Austria. The gorge was formed by the Myrabach, a small tributary of the Piesting at the foot of the Unterberg.

#### Kalkalpen

[http://www.nationalparkregion.com/](http://www.nationalparkregion.com/) Kalkalpen National Park, in the Pyhrn-Eisenwurzen region, opened to visitors in 1997 and extends into the high Alpine areas. It protects many endangered animals, including around 1,600 butterfly species. Its symbol is an owl. The visitor center in Molln, about 23 km south of Bad Hall, serves hikers, cyclists, horse riders and nature lovers. Routes are available year-round; the center opens from 10:00 to 18:00.
