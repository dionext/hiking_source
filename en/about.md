# About the project

Our project is about the world of hiking (trekking, backpacking, etc. — see the section ["Terminology"](terms)). We offer some [inspiring materials](hiking) explaining why this activity is so popular. Our main goal is to help beginners discover this remarkable world of physical activity. Here you can find reference and technical information to help you choose a destination, plan your trip and prepare for it. Experienced travelers may also find our project interesting.

Our project is still relatively young, and work on it is far from complete. Where our own materials are insufficient, we supplement them with links to external resources. In addition to providing original content, our project aggregates tourist information. We hope that with our help you will find the information you are looking for.

About our [geographical database](places/list).  
It is built by integrating information from Wikidata (Wikidata acts as a central repository for the structured data of its Wikimedia sister projects, including Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, Wikivoyage, Wiktionary, Wikisource, and others).  
Initially, we limited our information to Alpine countries (countries whose territory includes part of the Alps). In the future, we will try to expand our database to include other popular tourist destinations.

The database supports projects of the [Wikimedia movement](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Help:Wikimedia) around the world.
